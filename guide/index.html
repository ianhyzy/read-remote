Parts and tools

Parts

  • Seeed XIAO nRF52840
  • 402025 LiPo, 150 mAh, with protection board
  • 2 × 6×6×5 mm silent tact switch, silicone top
  • SS12D00G3 slide switch
  • 28–30 AWG stranded wire
  • Kapton tape, solder

Tools

  • Fine-tip iron, flux
  • Flush cutters, tweezers, wire strippers
  • Multimeter with continuity beep
  • Calipers
  • CA glue

LiPo safety. Never let the two battery leads touch. Cut and strip them one at a time and tape each end before touching the other. Keep the iron away from the pouch.

Flash and test the bare board

  1. Download the latest firmware artifact from the repo's Actions page and unzip it. You need read_remote.uf2.
  2. Plug the XIAO into your computer and double-press its reset button. A drive called XIAO-SENSE appears.
  3. Drag read_remote.uf2 onto the drive. It ejects itself and the board reboots.
  4. On the e-reader, pair Read Remote in Bluetooth settings.
  5. Touch tweezers across D0 and GND: the page turns forward. D1 to GND turns back.

Cut long, trim at assembly. The D0/D1 side of the board is the side the slide switch sits on in the case; GND is on the opposite side. The battery pads are labelled on the underside silkscreen; go by the labels, not by the diagram's positions.

Solder on the bench

Tact switches on the tray

Slide switch

The switch lies on its side in the case, handle out through the wall. Its pins have only 1 mm of room before a wall on the lid, so they get clipped short and folded flat against the switch. Straight pins hit that wall and the switch won't sit flat.

Battery

XIAO

The board rests on 2 mm rails along its long edges, so the underside joints and Kapton must stay under 1.5 mm tall and between the rails.

Bench test

If OFF does not cut power, the ON wire is on the wrong end pin. Move it to the other end pin.

Assemble

  1. Battery and board. Switch to OFF. Lay the battery flat in the bay at the far end, leads toward the board. Set the XIAO on its rails, components up, USB-C into the end opening. It is held by the end stops and the rounded corners.

  2. Knob and slide switch. From inside, push the knob's tile out through the side window and slide it to the USB end of the window. Set the switch handle to OFF, then press the handle into the knob's pocket with the ON end toward the buttons and the bent pins pointing down at the board. The switch hangs on the knob until the lid goes on.

  3. Tray. Lower the tray onto the ledge around the battery. Its front edge rests on the board's end stops. Tuck the wires down along the sides, clear of the snap-finger slots in the walls.

  4. Caps and lid. Put the lid face down on the bench and drop both caps into their windows, flange up. Turn the base over and lower it onto the lid. Check that the slide switch lines up between the two small walls on the lid, then press until all four fingers click.
